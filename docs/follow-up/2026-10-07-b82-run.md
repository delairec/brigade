## Problems to add to the plan

### F0

1. **A segment cut at the wall limit is not measured.** Claude Code writes its JSON result only at the end, so the
   cut loses it: segment 1 ran 47 min with no session, no steps and no usage. Fix: launch with
   `--session-id <uuid>` so the session is known before any cut, and on `wall_limit` fill `steps` and `usage` from the
   session transcript through the adapter's `transcripts` operation. Related to @TASK.B83, a different cause.
2. **A wall-time cut is recorded as `budget_reached`.** The ledger holds `signal.recorded budget_reached budget 75`
   for segment 1, which ended on the clock, not on the step cap. This contradicts DESC 3 of
   @DECISION.EveryRunIsHeldOnTheClockAtThirtySecondsPerStepOfItsCap, "the segment ends with outcome wall_limit,
   distinct from the step cap". Fix: a distinct signal carrying the limit in seconds.
3. **`brigade ledger` counts segments as runs.** "runs finished: done 3, failed 4, wall_limit 1" sums the 8
   `run.finished` events, one per segment: the run of B82 counts once as `wall_limit` and once as `done`. Fix: count
   runs and segments apart.
4. **The installed brigade runs a stale checkout.** `~/.local/bin/brigade` links to the main checkout, which stood at
   61100fd while `origin/main` was at 3deeea2, without the fix of B71 (#52); the run was launched from an extract of
   `origin/main` instead. Fix: `brigade doctor` warns when the checkout it runs from is behind its upstream. Check
   first whether @TASK.B45 already covers the install mode.

### ADD0 — post-MVP

5. **No way to hand a message to a running run.** A message for ada about the L016 errors of `main` could not reach
   the run; `claude -p` takes no input once launched. Fix: `brigade run note <run> <text>`, appended to the brief of
   the next segment or of a relaunch.
6. **`brigade runs list` shows `done` without saying the run was cut and resumed.** The detail is in `brigade.log`
   and the ledger only. Fix: the number of segments and the outcome of each in `runs list`.
7. **The installed brigade updates by hand.** Follows item 4: brigade updates itself, or installs from
   `dist/brigade.pyz` rather than from a clone.

## From the review of #56

What the review of #56 revealed about brigade itself, not defects of that pull request.

### F0

8. **The CI runs without awawa.** Every CI job skips 70 tests (`OK (skipped=70)` on main 3deeea2 and on #56), and
   no job runs `awawa fmt --check` or `awawa lint --strict`: #52 merged with two L016 that broke `lint --strict` on
   main. Fix: install awawa in the CI and add a corpus job.
9. **The 100 % coverage is not enforced.** `.coveragerc` sets `fail_under = 93`; the CI coverage job prints 94 %
   (job 112779485130); the 100 % of @TASK.B70 and SPEC 7 of @TASK.B82 holds only on a machine with awawa on PATH.
   Fix: once item 8 is done, `fail_under = 100`.
10. **A path is covered under Python 3.11 only.** The extra arguments of `brigade run` after its options
    (`brigade/cli.py:179-184`, `brigade/commands/run.py:52-53`) are reachable under 3.11 only: from 3.12 argparse
    gives those words to the `nargs="*"` positional. The coverage job runs on 3.11 alone. Fix: combine the coverage
    of both interpreters, or remove the path's dependence on the argparse version.
11. **Behaviour left unguarded by tests.** No test reads `brigade --help` or the help of a group, so the help strings
    the command records declare are unguarded; the validation of `runs logs --tail` and the message of `--since`
    are unasserted (both pre-exist B82); `brigade/commands/tick.py:128-133` sends any mode other than `once` and
    `dry-run` into the loop, so a lost `required=True` makes `brigade tick` loop forever, as a mutant of the
    implementing run did. Fix: tests for each, and an explicit refusal of an unknown mode.

### Found while launching the review round of #56
